Questions

Good to know

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We handle the design, domain, hosting, booking and payment setup. You only share your clinic details, photos and preferences.

Do patients need to download an app for video consultations?

No. Patients join the consultation from your website on a phone or laptop. The call stays on your website, not in another app.

Can patients pay before they book?

Yes. We can set up payment before booking, using options that suit your country, such as UPI and Google Pay in India or card payments.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. We can connect a domain you already own, or help you buy a new one, such as yourclinic.com.

Can I change things after the website is live?

Of course. Send us your changes on WhatsApp, such as new timings, a new doctor or new services, and we update the website for you.

Do you work with clinics outside the UAE?

Yes. We work online, so we can build websites for clinics and doctors in the UAE, India and beyond.
